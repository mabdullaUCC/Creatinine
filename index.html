Creatinine production

Continuous

Occurs spontaneously from creatine and phosphocreatine.

At rest, ATP phosphorylates creatine to replenish phosphocreatine. Creatinine forms continuously by spontaneous reactions.

Illustrative animation, not a quantitative kinetic model. Creatinine formation is greatly accelerated visually. Physiologically, about 1–2% of the creatine pool converts to creatinine per day; phosphocreatine also contributes and releases inorganic phosphate.
